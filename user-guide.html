Manage cities

Add

Follow another city: ellipsis menu → Add City... (⌘N).

Rename

Use names that mean something to you, like “🇨🇦 Eva” or “Work.” Right-click a city → Rename City. To bring its original name back, reopen Rename City and click ×.

Reorder and sort

Keep important cities at the top by dragging rows or using ⌘↑/⌘↓. For time or alphabetical order: ellipsis menu → Sort Cities.

Remove

Right-click → Remove City, or swipe left. Removed one by mistake? ⌘Z brings it back.

Compare time

We made comparing time quick. Click a city, type a time your way, and press Return. Chas shows the same moment in every city.

Your comparison stays fixed until you return to current time.

Type time your way

Start with compact input, or add a separator for precise minutes:

Colons, spaces, dots, and slashes can separate hours and minutes. A single minute digit means tens of minutes, so use two digits when precision matters.

In 12-hour · AM/PM format, enter a marker when needed: 930am, 3pm, or 3:30p. Without one, Chas uses that city's current AM/PM period. 12am is midnight; 12pm is noon. Enter only am or pm to switch the period while keeping the displayed date.

Today or tomorrow?

Chas picks the next time that clock time comes around in the city you edit. If it has already passed today, that’s usually tomorrow:

Add or subtract time

Start with + to move forward or − to move back from the time shown.

Current city timeInputResult

Adjust by one hour with ⇧↑/⇧↓ while a city is selected.

Return to current time

Choose Return to Current Time, press ⌘R, or click the counterclockwise-arrow button to resume all clocks.

Esc first cancels a name edit or closes city search. Otherwise, it resets an active time comparison, or cancels the time draft if no comparison is active.

More features

Home city and extra information

Your home city is the reference when you show hour or date differences beside the clocks. Without one, Chas uses the first city in your list.

To choose one, right-click a city → Set as Home City. Turn on extra information in Settings... → Extra info. +2 h means two hours ahead of home; -2 h means two hours behind.

Date if different shows a date only when the day differs from home. While comparing another time, it uses the city whose time you last changed. Hour differences still use your home city.

Use the widget

Keep your cities on your desktop or in Notification Center: Edit Widgets → Chas Cities.

The widget always shows current time, using the first cities in your list. Reorder them in Chas to choose what appears. Click the widget to open Chas.

Keep on Top

Choose Keep on Top from the ellipsis menu or the macOS View menu to keep the Chas window above ordinary windows. Choose it again to turn the option off. There is no keyboard shortcut.
