Privacy Policy

Your data stays with you. We want you to understand how Chas handles it, so here's everything in plain English.

1. No accounts or servers of our own. Chas doesn't require you to sign up or send us your data. We can't see your cities, their custom names, or your settings.

2. Everything stays on your Mac. Chas only saves what's needed to make the app work:

  • your cities, their custom names, and your home city;
  • your preferences: 12- or 24-hour format, seconds, appearance, language, and text size;
  • window size and position.

Desktop widgets use the same data.

3. The internet helps find cities and keep time zones up to date. When you search for a city, your search text is sent to Apple Maps to find matching locations. The coordinates of your selected city are sent to Apple to determine its time zone.

Chas never asks for your current location. Apple handles these requests under its Privacy Policy. We don't see them.

4. No ads, no tracking. Chas has no ads, trackers, or its own crash-reporting systems. We don't track what you do in the app or collect your personal data.

5. Crash reports come through Apple. If you have “Share with App Developers” enabled under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports.

These help us find and fix bugs without revealing who you are.

6. Your cities are your business. We don't analyze your city lists or share them with anyone. We don't even know who “Mom” is in your list or what time it is where she lives. And we certainly don't sell that information to travel agencies.
