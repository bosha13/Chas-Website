Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Chas is a world clock app for macOS. The app does not require an account, login, or any special configuration.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, sell, or share users' personal information, including children's personal information.

Chas does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party trackers. The app does not track user activity across other apps or websites.

City Search

When you search for a city, your search text is sent to Apple Maps services to find matching places and their time zones. This request is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy and is not sent to us.

Local Data

Chas stores your list of cities and app settings locally on your Mac so that the app and its widgets work correctly between launches. This data remains on your device and is not sent to us.
